15) Cyclobutadiene & cyclooctatetraene both appear cyclic, planar, & conjugated

Both molecules also appear to not follow Hückel’s rule. However cyclobutadiene is anti-aromatic while cyclooctatetraene is nonaromatic. Explain this discrepancy.

Show answer
Answer

Planar, cyclic, & conjugated rings that do not follow Hückel’s rule are generally extremely unstable because their last 2 e− end up in orbitals that do not provide any bonding stability. This generally makes molecules highly unstable and “antiaromatic”. Some molecules, especially larger ones can circumvent this issue by bending out of the plane & breaking planarity, resulting in them being classified as nonaromatic.

Cyclooctatetraene is able to break planarity as it is larger & more flexible thus it is nonaromatic. Cyclobutadiene is too small & rigid to bend thus it remains planar & anti-aromatic.
